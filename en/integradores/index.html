Frequently asked questions

Can I try Haila for free?

Yes. The integrator's first client is free for 12 months. The account is created after CNPJ confirmation and activation is automatic.

What is the monthly fee Haila charges for the platform?

The price depends on the plan, building size and selected modules. Talk to the commercial team for an updated proposal.

What kind of support will I have with my clients?

The integrator handles commercial support and first-level service; Haila provides platform technical support and follows the implementation.

How does the relationship between Haila and the integrator's clients work?

The client belongs to the integrator. Haila is the platform behind the operation, with the integrator's brand and commercial model in front.

Can I reuse existing infrastructure with Haila?

Yes, when compatible. Existing networks and equipment can be reused; compatibility is validated case by case during implementation.

Which equipment is approved?

The list is maintained by the technical team and changes over time. Confirm the specific model with the commercial or technical team.

Can I use only the QR Code intercom?

Yes. The QR Code intercom is an independent module and can be the first step, with other modules added later.

Does Haila have an integration API?

Yes. The platform exposes an API for integration with integrator and building systems. The scope is defined with the technical team.
