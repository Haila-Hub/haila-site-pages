Autonomous Building

Autonomous Building

122 autonomous buildings using Haila

A 360 solution for buildings looking for autonomy at low cost: residents register dependents, create invites with a selfie and answer the intercom in a single app that coexists with legacy analog infrastructure.

Live cameras, clips and DVR recordings are available in the resident and manager app, with AI assisting every step.

With the Resident app, residents are free to do what they need within the rules set by the building management.
The self-registration link enables autonomous onboarding: residents register and the manager decides whether approval is required.
