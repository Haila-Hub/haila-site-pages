The solution includes

Bookings

Smart booking management

Let clients book meeting rooms, workstations and auditoriums with custom rules and capacity control.

  • Bookings by time or period
  • Rules per space and capacity
  • Dependent registration
  • Digital keys linked to bookings
Portal

Autonomous booking portal

Give clients their own portal to check availability, book and track usage without depending on administration.

  • Client booking portal
  • Real-time availability
  • Prices per contract
  • Booking management by co-managers
Contracts

Custom contracts and plans

Create contract models with monthly credits, per-space limits and automatic consumption management.

  • Custom contracts per client
  • Monthly usage credits
  • Consumption and overage control
  • Commercial rules per space
Access

Autonomous access and digital intercom

Remove the need for a permanent reception and release spaces with digital keys, Bluetooth or face recognition.

  • Temporary digital keys
  • Bluetooth opening
  • Face recognition
  • Permissions synced with bookings
Clients

Client and company management

Centralize HUB companies, owners and users, with permissions and links aligned to contracts.

  • Company and client registration
  • Linked user management
  • Permissions per client
  • Centralized administration
Integration

IoT integration and automation

Connect the HUB digital management to physical infrastructure, integrating locks and access devices.

  • Smart lock integration
  • Haila-owned hardware
  • Booking-based access release
  • Platform and device synchronization
